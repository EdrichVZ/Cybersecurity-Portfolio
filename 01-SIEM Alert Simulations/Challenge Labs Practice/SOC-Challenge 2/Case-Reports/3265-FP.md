## Reason for Classifying as False Positive

The alert triggered due to multiple **denied connection attempts** from internal host `10.20.2.1` targeting multicast destination address `239.255.255.250` over TCP port `7680`.

TCP port `7680` directed to multicast group `239.255.255.250` is standard traffic generated by the **Windows Delivery Optimization (WDO)** service for local peer discovery and update distribution.

Although the traffic was dropped by firewall policy (`action: dropped`), this benign broadcast discovery mechanism is native to Windows operating systems and does not represent malicious lateral movement or threat activity.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **False Positive**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

The activity was identified as legitimate Windows Delivery Optimization traffic, with no evidence of malicious activity.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

Tune firewall rule `lateral-movement-prevent` on `FW-CHARON-01` to exclude local **TCP 7680 multicast discovery traffic (`239.255.255.250`)** from triggering excessive-deny alerts.

This should reduce recurring false positives generated by legitimate Windows Delivery Optimization activity.

---

## Attack Indicators

**None**

The activity was identified as standard, benign **Windows Delivery Optimization discovery traffic**.
