## Reason for Classifying as True Positive

The alert triggered due to repeated **denied connection attempts** originating from internal VPN IP `10.30.3.16` while probing internal targets, including ICMP traffic directed at `10.60.0.20` in the Development network.

Cross-referencing **Alert 3257** confirms that IP `10.30.3.16` was assigned to the VPN session established by external attacker IP `128.199.215.40` following the successful brute force of account `j.mitchell`.

This activity represents **post-compromise network reconnaissance and lateral movement attempts** originating from a compromised VPN session.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **True Positive**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

Per SOC policy, any active **lateral movement or network discovery attempts** originating from a compromised user account or VPN endpoint represent active post-exploitation activity and must be escalated to **Tier 2 / Incident Response**.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

* Immediately **terminate the active VPN session** associated with `10.30.3.16`.
* **Revoke all active credentials and sessions** for user `j.mitchell`.
* **Block external attacker IP** `128.199.215.40` on `FW-CHARON-01`.
* **Isolate target host** `10.60.0.20` and conduct forensic analysis to verify whether any unauthorized internal connections succeeded prior to the block/denial.
* Review firewall and network logs for additional internal targets that may have been probed from `10.30.3.16`.
