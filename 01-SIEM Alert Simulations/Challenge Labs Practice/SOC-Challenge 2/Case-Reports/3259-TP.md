## Reason for Classifying as True Positive

The alert triggered due to multiple **failed authentication attempts** originating from external source IP `180.101.88.223` via SSH and targeting the `sysadmin` account on `jumphost_01`.

Relevant authentication information:

* `auth_status: failure`
* `message: Failed password for sysadmin...`

This confirms **unauthorized external SSH brute-force activity** targeting the system.

Since all authentication attempts failed, **no successful unauthorized SSH session was established**.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **True Positive brute-force attempt**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

Per SOC policy, external brute-force attempts that fail and do not lead to a successful authentication do not require escalation.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

* **Block external source IP** `180.101.88.223` at the perimeter firewall.
* Ensure root/administrative SSH logins are restricted on `jumphost_01`.
* Enforce **key-based authentication** for SSH access.
* Continue monitoring `jumphost_01` for additional authentication attempts from the same or other external sources.
