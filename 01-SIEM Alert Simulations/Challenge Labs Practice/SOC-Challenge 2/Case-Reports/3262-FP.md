## Reason for Classifying as False Positive

The alert triggered due to repeated **dropped UDP packets** directed at multicast IP address `224.0.0.251` on port `5353` from internal host `10.20.2.25`.

The destination address `224.0.0.251` and UDP port `5353` correspond to standard **mDNS (Multicast DNS / Bonjour / ZeroConf)** local service discovery traffic, which is routinely generated by operating systems and network devices.

While the traffic was blocked by internal firewall policies (`action: dropped`), this benign broadcast discovery traffic does not represent malicious lateral movement or post-compromise activity.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **False Positive**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

The traffic was identified as standard mDNS service discovery activity and there was no evidence of malicious behavior.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

Review the firewall rule `lateral-movement-prevent` on `FW-CHARON-01` to filter out local multicast discovery protocols, specifically **mDNS (`224.0.0.251:5353`)**, from triggering high-severity excessive-deny alerts.

This could reduce recurring false-positive alerts caused by legitimate service discovery traffic.

---

## Attack Indicators

**None**

The activity was identified as standard operating system and network-device **mDNS service discovery traffic**.
