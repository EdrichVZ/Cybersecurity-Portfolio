## Reason for Classifying as False Positive

The alert triggered due to multiple **dropped connection attempts** originating from internal host `10.20.2.21` targeting local multicast address `239.255.255.250` over TCP port `7680`.

TCP port `7680` combined with multicast IP `239.255.255.250` corresponds to standard **Windows Delivery Optimization (WDO)** peer-to-peer update distribution traffic.

While the traffic was blocked by internal firewall policies (`action: dropped`), this benign broadcast communication is generated natively by **Windows OS background processes** and does not represent malicious lateral movement or post-compromise activity.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **False Positive**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

The traffic was identified as legitimate Windows Delivery Optimization communication and there was no evidence of malicious activity.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

* Adjust firewall rule `lateral-movement-prevent` on `FW-CHARON-01`.
* Alternatively, tune the SIEM alert correlation rule to ignore **TCP port 7680 multicast traffic (`239.255.255.250`)**.
* This would help prevent legitimate Windows Delivery Optimization traffic from generating recurring high-severity false-positive alerts.

---

## Attack Indicators

**None**

The activity was identified as standard, benign **Windows Delivery Optimization discovery traffic**.
