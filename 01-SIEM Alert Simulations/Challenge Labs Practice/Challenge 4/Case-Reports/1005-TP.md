According to the SOC handover notes, Yani Zubair is currently experimenting with the AtomicRed attack simulation framework, which is known to generate false positive alerts. Editing the `/etc/hosts` file is a standard administrative task often associated with configuring local DNS routing during such testing, confirming this as a benign, authorized action.

N/A (No escalation required). The activity represents authorized administrative configuration and simulation testing by the IT department.

### Recommended Remediation Actions
