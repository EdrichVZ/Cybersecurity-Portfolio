# Alert 8814: Email Sent to External Recipient with Suspicious Top-Level Domain (TLD)

## Incident Classification

**False Positive**

## Escalation Required?

**No**

## Case Report

### Time of Activity

`09/08/2026 17:34:49.503`

### Affected Entities

| Entity               | Details                                                    |
| :------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| **User Account**     | `h.harris@thetrydaily.thm` — Harper Harris (Legal Liaison) |
| **Target Recipient** | `john@iHateDaily.tech`                                     |
| **Direction**        | Outbound                                                   |

### Reason for Classifying as False Positive

The email was sent by **Harper Harris, a Legal Liaison at The Try Daily**, issuing a formal takedown request regarding defamatory content on an external site.

Although the alert was triggered by the recipient's suspicious top-level domain (`.tech`), the email represents **legitimate outbound business and legal activity** within the user's explicit job responsibilities, with no malicious intent present.

### Reason for Not Escalating the Alert

Escalation is not required because **no security policy was violated, no malicious activity occurred, and the action is confirmed as benign business operations**.

### Recommended Remediation Actions

* Close the alert as **False Positive**.
* Fine-tune the email security filter rules for suspicious TLDs to reduce noise on legitimate outbound legal communications.

## Attack Indicators

| **Subject Line**      | `Formal Takedown Request - Defamatory Content Concerning TheTryDaily` |

## Analyst Verdict

**Classification:** False Positive
**Escalation:** No

The suspicious `.tech` TLD triggered the alert, but the surrounding context confirms that the email was part of legitimate legal and business activity. No evidence of malicious activity was identified.
