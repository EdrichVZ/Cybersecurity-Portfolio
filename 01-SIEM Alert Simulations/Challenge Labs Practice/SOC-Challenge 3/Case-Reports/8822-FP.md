# Alert 8822: Email Sent to External Recipient with Suspicious Top-Level Domain (TLD)

## Incident Classification

**False Positive**

## Escalation Required?

**No**

## Case Report

### Time of Activity

`09/08/2026 17:53:28.593`

### Affected Entities

| Entity              | Details                                                    |
| :------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sender Account**  | `h.harris@thetrydaily.thm` — Harper Harris (Legal Liaison) |
| **Recipient Email** | `john@iHateDaily.tech`                                     |
| **Direction**       | Outbound                                                   |

### Reason for Classifying as False Positive

An outbound email was sent by internal user **Harper Harris** (`h.harris@thetrydaily.thm`) to an external recipient (`john@iHateDaily.tech`).

The email content is a **legitimate corporate legal communication** issuing a formal takedown request regarding defamatory content published on `iHateDaily.tech`.

Although the destination TLD (`.tech`) triggered the detection rule for suspicious or uncommon top-level domains, the email contains **no phishing indicators, malicious links, or rogue attachments**.

### Reason for Not Escalating the Alert

Escalation is not required as this is **legitimate corporate legal outbound correspondence** without any identified security risk or policy violation.

### Recommended Remediation Actions

* Close the alert as **False Positive**.
* Update the email detection rule to differentiate between suspicious inbound emails and legitimate outbound **administrative/legal communications** sent to external TLDs.

| **Attachment** | None                                                                          |

## Analyst Verdict

**Classification:** False Positive
**Escalation:** No

The alert was determined to be a **false positive triggered by the uncommon `.tech` TLD**. The email was legitimate outbound legal correspondence from an internal Legal Liaison to an external recipient. No phishing indicators, malicious links, rogue attachments, or other evidence of malicious activity were identified.
