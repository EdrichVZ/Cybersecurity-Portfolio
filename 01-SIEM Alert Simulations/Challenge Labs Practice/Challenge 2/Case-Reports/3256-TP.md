## Reason for Classifying as True Positive

The alert triggered due to multiple **failed authentication attempts** originating from external source IP `128.199.215.40` and targeting the user account `j.mitchell`.

This represents an **unauthorized external brute-force/password-guessing activity** attempting to compromise corporate VPN credentials.

All attempts were denied:

* `auth_status: failed`
* `session_status: denied`

This confirms that **no successful authentication occurred**.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **True Positive brute-force attempt**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

Per SOC rules, brute-force attempts that do not result in a successful login or subsequent post-exploitation actions do not require escalation.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

* Temporarily **block or apply rate-limiting** to source IP `128.199.215.40` on the perimeter firewall (`FW-CHARON-01`).
* Monitor `j.mitchell` for any subsequent **successful logins** or **account lockout events**.
* Continue monitoring VPN authentication logs for additional attempts from the same source IP.
