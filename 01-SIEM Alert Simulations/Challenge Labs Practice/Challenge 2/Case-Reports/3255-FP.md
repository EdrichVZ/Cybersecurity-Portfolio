## Reason for Classifying as False Positive

The alert triggered due to multiple outbound connections over **UDP port 1194 (OpenVPN)** from IP address `10.20.2.9`.

Cross-referencing company documentation confirms that IP `10.20.2.9` belongs to **Jack Carter (`j.carter`)**, an IT Support Specialist.

SOC handover documentation specifically notes that Jack Carter requested firewall authorization to access the **TryHackMe VPN** from his workstation for training/testing purposes.

Furthermore, the firewall action shows that the packets were **dropped (`action: dropped`)**, confirming that no unauthorized outbound VPN tunnel was established.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was determined to be **legitimate and authorized testing activity**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

The activity was authorized and no successful unauthorized connection was established.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

Review and approve/update the firewall rule request for Jack Carter's workstation (`10.20.2.9`) to permit outbound **TryHackMe VPN traffic over UDP port 1194**.

This would prevent legitimate testing traffic from being repeatedly dropped and reduce recurring false-positive alerts.

---

**None**

The activity was determined to be legitimate, authorized employee testing activity.
