### Reason for Classifying as False Positive

The alert triggered when user Rebecca Dawson opened a macro-enabled document (`Monthly_Sales.docm`) directly via Windows Explorer (`explorer.exe`).

Per company policy updates noted in the SOC Handover Notes, macro-enabled Office files (`*.docm`, `*.xlsm`) are prohibited unless explicitly approved by IT Security.

Because opening a legitimate internal sales document violates corporate compliance policy rather than indicating an external threat actor or malware execution, this event is classified as a false positive security alert.
