# Alert 8824: Email Sent to External Recipient with Suspicious Top-Level Domain (TLD)

## Incident Classification

**True Positive**

## Escalation Required?

**Yes**

## Case Report

### Time of Activity

`09/08/2026 17:59:35.593`

### Affected Entities

| Entity              | Details                                  |
| :------------------ | :--------------------------------------- |
| **Sender Account**  | `j.carter@thetrydaily.thm` — Jack Carter |
| **Recipient Email** | `gosalyn@duckworth.media`                |
| **Direction**       | Outbound                                 |

### Reason for Classifying as True Positive

An outbound email was sent by internal user **Jack Carter** (`j.carter@thetrydaily.thm`) to an external recipient (`gosalyn@duckworth.media`).

The body of the email contains **sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** and security verification details sent in response to an unverified external query ("Duck-umentary").

The sensitive information disclosed includes:

* **Full Name:** Jack Carter
* **Date of Birth:** March 15, 1987
* **Contact Number:** +1 555-782-4196
* **Mailing Address:** 123 Feather Lane, Lakeview, IL 60532
* **Mother's Maiden Name:** Whitmore
* **Favorite Childhood Pet:** Darkwing
* **City of Birth:** Boston

This represents an **unauthorized disclosure of sensitive personal and security verification information** to an unverified external recipient.

### Reason for Escalating the Alert

Escalation is required immediately due to the **unauthorized disclosure/exfiltration of PII and security challenge answers** via outbound email to an unverified external entity.

The exposed information could increase the risk of **social engineering, targeted spear-phishing, and account takeover**.

### Recommended Remediation Actions

* Immediately notify the **SOC incident response team** and Human Resources / Security Management.
* Intercept and reset credentials for `j.carter@thetrydaily.thm` across internal directories and review active security challenge/reset questions.
* Contact the user, **Jack Carter**, to provide immediate security awareness training regarding social engineering and PII disclosure.
* Apply outbound **Data Loss Prevention (DLP)** controls to flag or block emails containing common PII patterns or security question answers.

## Analyst Verdict

**Classification:** True Positive
**Escalation:** Yes

The alert was confirmed as a **True Positive involving unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information**. The internal user sent multiple pieces of PII and security verification information to an unverified external recipient. The combination of personal information and security challenge answers creates a potential risk of **social engineering and account compromise**, requiring immediate SOC escalation and appropriate containment measures.
