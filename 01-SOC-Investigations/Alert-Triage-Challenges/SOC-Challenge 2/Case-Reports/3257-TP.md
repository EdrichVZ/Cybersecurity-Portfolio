## Reason for Classifying as True Positive

The alert triggered following a series of **failed authentication attempts** from external source IP `128.199.215.40` targeting user `j.mitchell`.

The attempts resulted in a **successful VPN authentication**:

* `auth_status: success`
* `session_status: opened`

This indicates that the external brute-force attack **succeeded in compromising the user's VPN credentials** and resulted in unauthorized VPN access.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **True Positive successful brute-force attack**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

Per SOC policy, any successful brute-force attack resulting in **unauthorized access or compromised user credentials** represents a critical breach of perimeter defense and must be immediately escalated to **Tier 2 / Incident Response**.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

* Immediately **terminate the active VPN session** for assigned IP `10.30.3.16`.
* Force a **password reset** and revoke all active authentication tokens for account `j.mitchell`.
* **Block external source IP** `128.199.215.40` on `FW-CHARON-01`.
* Conduct a **forensic audit of network traffic** originating from assigned VPN IP `10.30.3.16` for signs of post-exploitation or lateral movement.
* Review the compromised account for any suspicious activity following the successful authentication.
