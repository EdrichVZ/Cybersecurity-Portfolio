## Reason for Classifying as False Positive

The alert triggered due to multiple **outbound connections over UDP port 1194 (OpenVPN)** from IP `10.20.2.9`.

Internal documentation confirms that IP `10.20.2.9` belongs to **Jack Carter (`j.carter`)**, an IT Support Specialist.

Per SOC handover notes, Jack Carter requested firewall authorization to access the **TryHackMe VPN** from his workstation for training and testing purposes.

The firewall action indicates that the connections were **blocked (`action: dropped`)**, confirming that no unauthorized outbound tunnel was established.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **False Positive** and legitimate authorized testing activity.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

The activity was authorized and no successful unauthorized connection was established.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

Review the pending firewall rule request for Jack Carter's host (`10.20.2.9`) to permit outbound **TryHackMe VPN traffic on UDP port 1194**.

This would prevent duplicate dropped-connection alerts during authorized lab testing.
