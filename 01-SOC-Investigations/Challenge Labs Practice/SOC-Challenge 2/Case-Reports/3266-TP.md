## Reason for Classifying as True Positive

The alert triggered due to multiple **failed VPN authentication attempts** originating from external source IP `190.104.25.221` and targeting the generic account `consultant`.

This confirms **external brute-force authentication activity** targeting IPsec VPN access.

All login attempts failed:

* `auth_status: failed`
* `session_status: denied`

This confirms that **no unauthorized access was granted**.

Based on the available evidence, the activity was classified as a **True Positive brute-force attempt**.

---

## Reason for Escalating the Alert

**N/A — No escalation required.**

Per SOC policy, external brute-force attempts that fail and do not lead to a successful authentication do not require escalation.

---

## Recommended Remediation Actions

* **Block external source IP** `190.104.25.221` on perimeter firewall `FW-CHARON-01`.
* **Disable or rename generic account names**, such as `consultant`, to minimize potential password-spraying and brute-force targets.
* Continue monitoring the account for any subsequent successful authentication attempts.
